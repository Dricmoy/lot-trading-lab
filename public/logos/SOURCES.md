# Stock marks

Apple, NVIDIA, Tesla, Google, Coinbase and Robinhood marks come from the Simple Icons package installed as a development dependency. The source vector shapes are copied unchanged to the matching ticker files. See https://simpleicons.org and https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons for source and brand information.

Microsoft and Amazon use their respective public site favicons, downloaded from https://www.microsoft.com/favicon.ico and https://www.amazon.com/favicon.ico on October 5, 2026.

All names and marks identify the companies whose stocks are represented in the simulator. They do not indicate an affiliation with Lot.
